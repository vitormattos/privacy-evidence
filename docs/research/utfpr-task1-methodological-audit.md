## 1. Scope, sources and decision rule

The one-page UTFPR **`Tarefa-1.pdf`** rubric identifies six distinct syllabus axes: (1) qualitative interviews and focus groups; (2) coding and thematic analysis; (3) Grounded Theory; (4) survey design, sampling and validation; (5) exact and differentiated replication; and (6) replication/laboratory packaging following Carver. `Planejamento.pdf` places these topics in the 05/10 and 07/10/2026 classes. Both are course materials supplied to the researcher, **not** publicly redistributable research-run artifacts and **not** committed here.

The governing scientific design is [the contribution model](research-contribution.md), [research RQs and evidence map](research-model.md), [methodological stack](methodology.md), [standards checklist](standards-compliance.md), [manuscript](../../paper/manuscript.md) and [manuscript evidence map](../../paper/evidence-map.md). The actual empirical center remains **measurement attrition and evidence provenance in empirical web research**, operationalized as an observational real-population evaluation of a Design Science / Engineering Research artifact. Interviewing software professionals, building inductive theory or measuring perceptions of tool usability are **not** substitutes for the frozen propositions.

**Evidence vocabulary (non-interchangeable):**

- **Defined/designed:** a method, protocol or instrument is described; no observations necessarily exist.
- **Implemented/tested:** software, fixtures or CI exercise a capability; this does **not** establish empirical use by participants.
- **Collected:** real study observations exist for a declared ResearchRun or a genuine participant.
- **Analyzed/frozen:** declared analysis was executed and the output/provenance was preserved.
- **Independently reproduced/replicated:** the independently executed attempt and its deviations are documented; a public repository, hash or green CI is not sufficient.
- **Not applicable (N/A):** the technique does not answer a current paper RQ without a separate research question/design.
- **Blocked/pending:** a specified human, publication, comparison or observation gate remains unmet.

Avoid a binary “applied/not applied” label that hides the difference between a programmed feature, a designed study and a completed empirical study.

### Replication taxonomy without changing `reproducibility.md`

Use two orthogonal distinctions rather than equating “same script” with replication:

1. **Old data vs new observations:** same data and analytic pipeline = **reproduction**; new acquisition/data and repeat analysis = **replication** (ACM SIGSOFT Replication Standard). A clean checkout of an original frozen run is not a newly sampled population.
2. **What changed:** hold or vary question, method/operationalization, context/population, personnel, tools and measurement window. The ACM standard distinguishes **exact** (same question/method/context), **methodological** (same question/method, new context) and **conceptual** (same question with a changed method/context). Gómez et al. discuss differentiated replication types; their terminology and the rubric's “operational” label should not be collapsed into automatic equivalences. For a temporally unstable live Web, exact identity of observed bytes is unattainable; record what the **design** held constant and what inevitably varied. Brooks et al. similarly warn against literal exactness.

A future #201 second population, if measured under unchanged core constructs, is a **context-varied / methodological-style replication and transferability test**, not an “exact” repeat, and not proof of generalizability by itself. Any altered evidence operationalization demands a declared protocol-version deviation and re-examination of comparability. A WEC alternative-tool baseline has its own CQ1–CQ5 and must not be promoted to a successful conceptual replication of RQ1–RQ5 without such a design.

| Claim/tempting reading | Strongest rival or bias | Evidence that could discriminate / present limit |

**Researcher positionality:** the artifact author is also its implementer and the analyst of an IPB-origin case. This dual role makes favorable instrument interpretation, hidden manual setup and selective error inspection credible rival explanations, not merely theoretical cautions. Disclose it; separate final detector tuning from the evaluation partition; prefer blinded human references and independent reproduction.

**Inference hierarchy:** source row accounting is stronger than an assumed causal reason for attrition; sensitivity arithmetic is stronger than any inferred “true” missing values; published interface code is weaker than observed human usability; successful automated tests are weaker than non-implementer reproduction; one context is weaker than cross-context transfer evidence. Absent evidence stays **unknown/pending**, never silently negative.

## 5. Validity, ethics and data governance

- **Construct validity:** observational privacy signals do not establish legal/organizational compliance. Missing acquisition is not absence. Distinguish site, canonical unit, evidence item, human label and organization.
- **Internal validity:** this is an observational case and deterministic sensitivity contrast, **not** a randomized causal experiment. DNS/HTTP categories and denominator changes cannot be framed as causal estimates. Instrument and selection rules may affect outcomes.
- **Conclusion validity:** rare signals, repeated observations per site/host, selective measurability, multiple tests, class imbalance, partial coverage and missing outcomes constrain inferential language. The frozen #194 Holm correction and limitations remain unchanged.
- **External validity:** IPB frame selection is contextual; the 12-site WEC list is deliberately heterogeneous, **not probability sampling**; the pilot and any convenience participants cannot support population-level usability prevalence. RQ5 is conditional.
- **Temporal / reliability limits:** version browser/runner/time, hold target list constant in matched comparisons, and distinguish observed website change from acquisition/tool drift.
- **Human research safeguards:** before interviewing, surveying or observing identifiable participants as research subjects, verify the applicable UTFPR research-ethics/consent requirements and data-retention policy. Pseudonyms, restricted access, voluntariness and minimal recording are required design considerations, not certifications already received. Group sessions expose participants to one another and must not contaminate independent annotation.
- **Open-science boundary:** follow [data-policy.md](data-policy.md). A package can provide verified hashes, manifests, synthetic fixtures, shareable aggregates and exact commands while keeping copyrighted raw pages, cookies, screenshots and incidental personal data restricted. Do not interpret non-redistribution as permission to invent/reconstruct unavailable source bytes.

No additional data collection, human contact, lab-package publication or original result recalculation is authorized by this audit alone.
