# GDPR public-evidence profile

Profile version: **1.0.0-draft**

## Authoritative basis

Regulation (EU) 2016/679:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679

Primary observable disclosure dimensions come from Articles 12–14, with DPO applicability/contact context from Article 37 and rights context from Articles 15–22.

## Observable dimensions

Articles 13/14 identify public-notice categories that may be measurable, including:
- controller identity/contact;
- DPO contact where applicable;
- purposes;
- legal basis;
- recipients/categories;
- international transfers where applicable;
- retention period/criteria;
- data-subject rights and related information.

## Limits

The profile does not establish:
- that a declared legal basis is legally correct;
- that DPO designation is mandatory for the entity;
- that disclosed retention matches internal practice;
- that rights requests are operationally fulfilled;
- organization-wide compliance.

Missing evidence on the sampled resource is reported as missing public evidence under the protocol, not as an automatic legal violation.
