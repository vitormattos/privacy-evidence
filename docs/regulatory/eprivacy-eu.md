# EU cookie/ePrivacy observable profile

Profile version: **1.0.0-draft**

## Authoritative basis

- Directive 2002/58/EC Article 5(3), as amended:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32002L0058
- Regulation (EU) 2016/679 consent conditions where applicable.
- EDPB consent guidance.

Article 5(3) concerns storage/access to information on terminal equipment, subject to its conditions and strictly-necessary exception.

## Observable browser experiment

Independent clean contexts can observe:
1. initial navigation before interaction;
2. cookies/storage/network requests;
3. available consent controls;
4. state after reject;
5. separate clean context and state after accept.

## Limits

The crawler can observe storage/access and interface behavior, but determining whether a specific item is “strictly necessary” may require contextual/legal adjudication.

National transposition and jurisdiction-specific requirements remain explicit applicability considerations.
